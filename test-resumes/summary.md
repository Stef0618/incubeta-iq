## Quality Notes

**Outstanding (5):** Deep SA360 hands-on expertise, enterprise retail spend ($15M+), strong client-facing track record, consultative sales or account ownership, and 6+ years of directly relevant experience.

**Great (10):** Solid SA360 experience with retail accounts, clear client-facing responsibilities, and 4-7 years of relevant experience. May lack one standout element (scale, leadership, or technical depth) compared to outstanding candidates.

**Good (25):** Meets minimum qualifications with SA360 exposure and digital advertising background. May have gaps such as limited hands-on SA360 depth, limited client-facing experience, non-retail verticals, or shorter tenure. Qualifiable but not slam-dunk hires.

**Poor (10):** Either lacks required experience (entry-level, wrong field, no SA360 or digital advertising background) or the resume itself is low quality (formatting issues, unprofessional tone, grammatical errors, or vague/unsubstantiated claims).
